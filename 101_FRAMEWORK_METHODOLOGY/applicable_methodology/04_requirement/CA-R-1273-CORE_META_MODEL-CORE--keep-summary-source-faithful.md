# Summary

Keep Summary Source-Faithful

## Scope

the faithfulness of an Atom's Summary **to** its authoritative content.

## Claim

a Summary **must** preserve the intent **and** applicability of its finalized primary contribution under CA-R-1465, **without** adding, broadening, narrowing, **or** contradicting its contribution. brevity **may** omit supporting detail, but **must not** change what the Atom is about **or** add a conclusion absent from that contribution. for RMED, source faithfulness is evaluated against the Claim read within its Scope, **not** against either section alone.
