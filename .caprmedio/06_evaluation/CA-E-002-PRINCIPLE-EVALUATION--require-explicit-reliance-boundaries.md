# Require explicit reliance boundaries

Every governed use of authority, Method, Implementation, Evaluation, Delivery, or Ops conclusions must state the evidence and material uncertainty under which reliance is permitted and the condition that stops, degrades, blocks, or reopens that reliance. A missing, unknown, or contradictory required input fails closed at the affected use.
