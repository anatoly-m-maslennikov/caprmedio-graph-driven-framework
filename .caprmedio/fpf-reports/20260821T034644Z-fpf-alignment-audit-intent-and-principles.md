### Semantic blocker BL-D001-OBLIGATION-ASSERTION

Verdict: **unsupported**.

Confidence: 100%.

Current human claim:

“Within its declared operating prerequisite envelope, CAPRMEDIO must provide replaceable technical realizations.”

Current formal claim:

\[
\forall r\in T:
\operatorname{ReplaceableWithin}(r,\Pi(r))
\iff
\exists m\in M_R:
\operatorname{AvailableWithin}(m,\Pi(r))
\land
\operatorname{CanProduceOrSelect}(m,Q_r)
\]

The equivalence defines ReplaceableWithin. It does not require either side to be true. A model with no available replacement Method and no replaceable realization satisfies the formula, while violating the human Principle. This is visible in CA-D-001 lines 12-45.

FPF A.7 requires the formal representation to preserve the governed claim rather than gain force merely from formal notation; its strict-distinction Solution is at A.7 lines 77-89. A.15 separately identifies a Method as a reusable way of doing and Capability as an ability envelope at lines 146-157. The present formula correctly uses Method for the criterion but omits the required capability assertion.

Recommended repair:

1. Retain the current equivalence as the definition of ReplaceableWithin.
2. Add the universal obligation:

\[
\forall r\in T:\operatorname{ReplaceableWithin}(r,\Pi(r))
\]

Equivalent compact form:

\[
\forall r\in T:
\exists m\in M_R:
\operatorname{AvailableWithin}(m,\Pi(r))
\land
\operatorname{CanProduceOrSelect}(m,Q_r)
\]

Rationale: the definition explains what counts as replaceable; the universal assertion expresses that CAPRMEDIO must actually provide that capability for every technical realization in scope.
