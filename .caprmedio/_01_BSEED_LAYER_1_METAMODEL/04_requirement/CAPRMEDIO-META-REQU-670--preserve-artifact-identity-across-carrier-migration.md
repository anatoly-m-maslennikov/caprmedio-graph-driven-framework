# Preserve Artifact identity across Carrier migration

An Artifact retains its stable identity across a Carrier move or format-preserving migration only while its governed meaning and identity remain unchanged; a physical address, filename, extension, digest, or Git commit may resolve a Revision but never replaces Artifact identity.
