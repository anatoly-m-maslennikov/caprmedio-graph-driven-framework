# Use Plan Type lifecycles

CAPRMEDIO applies Type-specific Plan lifecycles. An `action_policy` follows the PRMEDO authority lifecycle and governs Actors and actions rather than forming an action-point collection. A Development Backlog may remain active; Version, Change, and Refactoring Plans remain action-only and structurally single-scope and terminate in `done/` or `archive/` as applicable. Findings, alternatives, interpretation, and rationale remain Analysis rather than Plan content.
