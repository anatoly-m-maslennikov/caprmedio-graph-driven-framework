# Require falsifiable claims

Every governed CAPRMEDIO claim must state an observable condition or boundary by which it can be shown false, unsatisfied, or outside its applicability. Evidence that can only confirm the preferred interpretation is insufficient when a reasonable disconfirming check exists.
