# Preserve discipline-independent semantics

CAPRMEDIO must maintain one discipline-independent canonical semantic model. Extensions and Project Adaptations may adapt terminology, Artifact Types, Methods, Evaluation, and Implementation only through declared mappings that preserve canonical meanings.
