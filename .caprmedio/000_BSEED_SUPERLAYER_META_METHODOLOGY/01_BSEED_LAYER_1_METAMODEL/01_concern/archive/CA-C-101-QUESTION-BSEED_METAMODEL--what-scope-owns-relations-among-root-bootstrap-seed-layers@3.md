# What scope owns relations among root Bootstrap Seed Layers?

METAMODEL, SEMANTICS, and GOVERNANCE are peer ordered Scope Units with no registered Structural parent. Their cross-unit relational Atoms therefore have no narrowest common Scope Unit, while CAPRMEDIO explicitly does not own meaning bounded entirely outside CAPRMEDIO.

The metamodel must establish where such a relational Atom is owned without silently making CAPRMEDIO the Bootstrap Seed's Structural parent or placing the Atom inside one endpoint it also governs. Demand For Atoms are outside this Question because each Demand For Atom belongs to its current Scope Unit and names another Scope Unit only as its target.
