# Allow proposal of PRMEDO change from active content roles

Concern, Analysis, action-point Plan, and native Implementation may propose a PRMEDO authority change; only Operator-approved PRMEDO authority establishes that change. A tier-classified `action_policy` Plan is itself PRMEDO authority rather than a proposal carrier merely because its Content role is Plan.
