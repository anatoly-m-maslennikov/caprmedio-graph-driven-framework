# Reserve Principle-conflict resolution to the Operator

Only the Operator may resolve a conflict between active Project Principles. CAPRMEDIO may detect, analyze, and propose resolutions for that conflict but must not choose one. A conflict that does not involve two active Project Principles may be resolved automatically only when applicable authority explicitly permits it and the configured confidence requirements are satisfied.
