# Register Content-role identity letters

GOV registers the Content-role letters `C`, `A`, `P`, `R`, `M`, `E`, `D`, `I`, and `O` for Concern, Analysis, Plan, Requirement, Method, Evaluation, Delivery, Implementation, and Ops respectively. The letter is a filename classification component for a draft and becomes the middle component of `<PREFIX>-<CONTENT_ROLE_LETTER>-<NUMBER>` only when operator acceptance assigns the complete Atom ID.
