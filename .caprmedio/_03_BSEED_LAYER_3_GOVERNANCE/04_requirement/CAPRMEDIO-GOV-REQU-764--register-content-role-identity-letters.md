# Register Content-role identity letters

GOV registers the Content-role letters `C`, `A`, `P`, `R`, `M`, `E`, `D`, `I`, and `O` for Concern, Analysis, Plan, Requirement, Method, Evaluation, Delivery, Implementation, and Ops respectively. The letter constrains the middle component of the assigned `atom_id` value `<PREFIX>-<CONTENT_ROLE_LETTER>-<NUMBER>`. A draft filename carries the applicable role letter as classification but, without the number and `atom_id` property, that partial prefix is not an Atom ID.
