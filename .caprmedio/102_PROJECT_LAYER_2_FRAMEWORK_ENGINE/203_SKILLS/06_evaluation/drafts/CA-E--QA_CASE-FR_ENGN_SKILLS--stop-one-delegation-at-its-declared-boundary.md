# Stop one delegation at its declared boundary

Given a Skill delegation with an explicit target, input, authority boundary, expected result, and stop condition, make the delegated capability reach that stop condition without completing the larger task. Verify that the Skill stops or escalates as declared and does not expand authority, invent missing input, or continue through an unrelated capability.

Candidate alignment: CA-E-001, CA-E-002, CA-R-004, CA-R-846, CA-R-861.
