# Separate structural ownership from cross-unit flow

Dependencies, semantic control, authority inputs, realization flows, information or result flows, lifecycle succession, and workflow order connect Structural units through explicit non-ownership relations without changing their Structural parents or scope owners. Local order constrains only relation families that explicitly register that ordering domain and never creates an edge by itself.
