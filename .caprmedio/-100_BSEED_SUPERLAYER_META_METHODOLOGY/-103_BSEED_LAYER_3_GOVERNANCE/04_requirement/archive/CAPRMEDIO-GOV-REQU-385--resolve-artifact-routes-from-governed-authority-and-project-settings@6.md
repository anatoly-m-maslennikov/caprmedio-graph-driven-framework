CAPRMEDIO resolves Content roles, Artifact Types, class short names, semantic routes, carriers, and persistence behavior from active GOVERNANCE authority, applies operator selections from the Project Configuration Atom, and reads derived structural context from current Project Graph State Projections. Writers and validators fail closed for unknown, disabled, stale, multiply mapped, or ambiguous classifications.
