# Keep Project Adaptation authority out of settings

Project Adaptation and Extension meaning must be owned by their applicable Atoms. The Project Configuration Atom may select, disable, and parameterize them, and Project Graph State Projections may expose the effective result, but neither may redefine or amend their governed meaning.
