# Calibrate guidance to current adequacy

CAPRMADIO Skills and their prompts provide only the guidance necessary to meet the operator-defined adequacy threshold under the current model, provider, host, task, risk, and Assurance conditions, increasing or reducing guidance when evidence changes those conditions.
