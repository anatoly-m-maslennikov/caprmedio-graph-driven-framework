# Write context-complete minimal Atom prose

Every Atom must use the least wording from which its intended reader can
recover its intended use, scope, boundaries, and role-specific atomic unit
without guessing or consulting a Projection.

Atom prose uses canonical terms and the applicable role-specific statement
form. It references governed meanings instead of restating them and omits
facts already determined by the carrier address, frontmatter, or direct
relations. Concision must not hide a material actor, modality, target,
Applicability, source, uncertainty, inclusion boundary, exclusion boundary,
or stop condition.
