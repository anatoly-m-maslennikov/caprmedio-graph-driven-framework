# Require falsifiable claims and stop conditions

Every empirical CAPRMADIO Claim identifies an observable condition that could
show it to be false. Evidence capable only of confirming the preferred
interpretation is insufficient when a reasonable disconfirming check is
available.

Other governed meanings use their own checkability boundary. A Requirement
states satisfaction and non-satisfaction conditions; a definition states an
inclusion and exclusion test; evidential support, currentness, and
Applicability remain separate determinations.

Every use that accepts work, selects a Method, relies on an Implementation,
passes Assurance, declares Delivery or release readiness, or draws an Ops
conclusion identifies its acceptance or reliance boundary, required evidence,
material uncertainty, and stop, degrade, block, or reopen condition. If the
applicable check cannot be stated, the matter remains a Concern or explicit
assumption and cannot silently carry normative or assurance force. A missing,
unknown, or contradictory required input fails closed at the affected use.
