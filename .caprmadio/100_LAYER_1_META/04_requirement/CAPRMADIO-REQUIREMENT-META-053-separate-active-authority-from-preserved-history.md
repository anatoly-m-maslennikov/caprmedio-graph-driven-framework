# Separate active authority from preserved history

CAPRMADIO's durable control plane distinguishes accepted current project authority, applicable current Projections, and operative governance. Archives preserve accepted historical Atoms without making them active authority. Completed Plans under `done/` preserve executed planning history without remaining active work. Future intentions remain outside active authority in governed future-planning surfaces, and unaccepted exploration remains outside governed state.
