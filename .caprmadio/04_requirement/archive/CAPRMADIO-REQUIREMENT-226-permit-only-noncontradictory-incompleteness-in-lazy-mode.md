# Permit only noncontradictory incompleteness in lazy mode

A scope in lazy authority mode may admit orphan Cores or Standards and incomplete downward coverage, but its active authority must remain conflict-free, its operative graph must remain acyclic, every edge must follow the permitted tier and structural direction, and every operative relation target must be active.
