# Define the DELIVERY Layer scope and Contracts

PROJECT assigns DELIVERY the packaging, environment, deployment, release, and
publication realization of the project. DELIVERY consumes applicable PROJECT,
META, GOV, SPEC, and IMPLEMENTATION authority and supplies released units and
their environment identity to OPS.
