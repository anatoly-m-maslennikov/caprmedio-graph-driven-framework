# Register Refactoring Plan as a Plan subtype

GOV registers `refactoring_plan` as a direct subtype of the internal Plan Atom Type. A Refactoring Plan contains the action points for one bounded refactoring scope and their completion or abandonment conditions; associated rationale, required outcomes, technical approaches, assurance criteria, and delivery rules remain independently governed by their owning Content roles.
