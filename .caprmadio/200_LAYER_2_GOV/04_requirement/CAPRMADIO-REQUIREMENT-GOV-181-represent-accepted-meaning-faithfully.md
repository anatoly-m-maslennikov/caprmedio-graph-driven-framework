# Represent accepted meaning faithfully

Every governed carrier represents accepted semantic authority deterministically and human-readably without adding, removing, duplicating, or changing its meaning. Carrier identity, filename, placement, frontmatter, relation encoding, and generated presentation may make that authority addressable and inspectable, but cannot become a second semantic owner or silently alter the accepted claim.
