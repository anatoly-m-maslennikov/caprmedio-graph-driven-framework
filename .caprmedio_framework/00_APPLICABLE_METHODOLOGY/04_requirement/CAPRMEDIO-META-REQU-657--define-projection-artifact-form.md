# Define Projection Artifact form

A Projection is a non-authoritative generated view reproducibly derived from an exact declared frontier of Atom revisions and Journal records. The source Atoms and Journals remain the sole owners of the governed meaning and history rendered by the Projection. A Projection never becomes authority, and its generation mechanism does not alter this status.
