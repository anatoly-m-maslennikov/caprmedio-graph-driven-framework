# Prefer positive normative statements

Normative Atom prose states required presence or behavior positively whenever an equivalent positive formulation preserves the complete meaning. Negative formulation is reserved for genuine prohibitions, exclusions, absence constraints, or cases where a positive rewrite would lose or obscure the governed distinction.
