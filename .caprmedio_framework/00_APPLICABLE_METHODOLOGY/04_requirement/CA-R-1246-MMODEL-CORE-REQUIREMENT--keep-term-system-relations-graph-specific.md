# Keep Term-System Relations Graph-Specific

the Term System **must not** treat INSTANCE_OF **or** **any** relation from another CAPRMEDIO Graph as SUBTYPE_OF, IS_BORNE_BY, **or** IS_ALLOWED_VALUE_OF.
