# Keep Project Adaptation authority out of settings

Project Adaptation and Extension meaning MUST be owned by their applicable Atoms. The Project Configuration Atom MAY select, disable, and parameterize them, and Project Scope Unit Graph Projections MAY expose the effective result, but neither MAY redefine or amend their governed meaning.
