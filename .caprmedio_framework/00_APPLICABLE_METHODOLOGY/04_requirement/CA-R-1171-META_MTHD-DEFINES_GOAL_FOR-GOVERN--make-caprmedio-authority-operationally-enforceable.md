# Make CAPRMEDIO Authority Operationally Enforceable

GOVERNANCE **must** make CAPRMEDIO authority operationally enforceable through faithful encoding, validation, traceability, **and** reproducible maintenance **without** transferring authority to **any** mechanism **or** Projection.
