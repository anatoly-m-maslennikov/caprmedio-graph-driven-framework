# Derive Navigation Projections from the CCE Claim

**to** derive an Atom's navigation Projections, the Generator **must** read the complete Claim **and** Claim Scope, derive one concise source-faithful Summary, **and** derive **every** other requested Projection directly from the Claim **and** Claim Scope rather than from the Summary.
