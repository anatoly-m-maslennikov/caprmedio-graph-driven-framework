# Author one CCE Claim and derived Summary

to author one CAPRMEDIO Atom Claim, the Author must perform all of:

1. select one governed meaning that must be accepted, replaced, and retired together.
2. use one clause or one explicitly grouped logical composition for that governed meaning.
3. move every independently replaceable component to another Atom.
4. select exactly one Claim Scope through one atomic or composite Scope Expression.
5. write the complete Claim in human-readable CAPRMEDIO Controlled English.
6. derive one concise Summary from the Claim and Claim Scope.
7. derive the filename Summary slug and H1 from the Summary.
8. derive every Translation from the complete Claim and Claim Scope and not from the Summary.
