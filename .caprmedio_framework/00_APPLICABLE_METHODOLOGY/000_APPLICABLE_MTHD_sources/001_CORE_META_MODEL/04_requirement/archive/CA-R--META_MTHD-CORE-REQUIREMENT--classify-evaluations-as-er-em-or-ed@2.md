# Classify Evaluations as E_R, E_M, or E_D

E_R, E_M, **and** E_D **must** qualify Evaluation Atoms whose Evaluation Target Content Role is Requirement, Method, **and** Delivery, respectively, without changing Evaluation as their single Content Role.
