# Defer the replacement relation pair

GOVERNANCE defers formal `replaced_by` and `replacement_of` relation realization. Until it is separately admitted, replacement events record explicit predecessor and successor Atom IDs only in the archival Journal event and do not create direct relation payloads or inverse views.
