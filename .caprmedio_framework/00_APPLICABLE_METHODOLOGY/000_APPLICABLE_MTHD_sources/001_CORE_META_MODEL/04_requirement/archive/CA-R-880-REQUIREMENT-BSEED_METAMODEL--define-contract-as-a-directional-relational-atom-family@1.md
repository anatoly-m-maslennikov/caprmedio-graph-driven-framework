# Define Contract as a directional relational-Atom family

Contract is the family of role-classified relational Atoms whose independently governed primary meaning controls a directional relationship between exact project-graph endpoints. Contract is neither a Content role nor a universal Atom Type: every Contract retains exactly one ordinary Content role and one Type admitted within that role.

Atoms in different Content roles may therefore be Contracts without merging Requirement, Method, Evaluation, Delivery, Ops, or other role meanings.
