# Register Plan Types

GOVERNANCE registers `development_backlog`, `version_plan`, `change_plan`, and `refactoring_plan` as internal action-point Plan Types, and registers `action_policy` as the internal tier-eligible Plan Type for authority that governs who may perform or authorize actions. Its canonical class short name is `action_policy`.
