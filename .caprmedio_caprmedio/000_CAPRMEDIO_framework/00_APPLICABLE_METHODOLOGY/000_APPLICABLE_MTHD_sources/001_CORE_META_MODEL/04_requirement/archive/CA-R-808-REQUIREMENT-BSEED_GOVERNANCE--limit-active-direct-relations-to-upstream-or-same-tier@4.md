# Apply relation direction within its ordering domain

Every declared relation must satisfy the target position and ordering domain registered for its relation family. Normative-authority relations use the global-tier and authority hierarchy, temporal relations use lifecycle succession, realization relations use realization order, dependency relations use dependency order, and other admitted domains use their own registered order. No universal upstream or downstream rule may be inferred across different ordering domains, and an inverse-derived view does not create another declared edge.
