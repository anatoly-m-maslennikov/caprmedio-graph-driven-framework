# Validate Principle Content-role semantics

classify an Operations Principle **only** **when** its meaning defines operational behavior **or** who **may** participate **in**, perform, **or** authorize governed actions; classify a Requirement **or** Delivery Principle **only** **when** its meaning states what CAPRMEDIO provides **or** its Carrier boundary; **and** classify a Method **or** Evaluation Principle **only** **when** its meaning states an Implementation choice/convention **or** a check of accepted Spec authority. intended Task **or** Objective content belongs **to** Plan. the paraphrase is a semantic test of the complete Claim **and** does **not** require a literal wording template **or** assign a role merely because the Claim mentions an Actor **or** Operation.
