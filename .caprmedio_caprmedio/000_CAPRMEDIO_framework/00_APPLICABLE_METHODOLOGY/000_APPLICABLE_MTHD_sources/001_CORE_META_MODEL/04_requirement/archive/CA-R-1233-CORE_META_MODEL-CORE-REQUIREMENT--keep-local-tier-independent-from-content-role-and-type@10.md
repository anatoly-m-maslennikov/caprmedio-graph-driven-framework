# Keep Local Tier Independent from Content Role and Type

an Atom's Local Tier **must** remain a distinct classification coordinate from its Content Role **and** Type; the coordinates are **not** aliases for one another.

- applicable authority **may** constrain their admitted combinations, including the Operations tier mapping under CA-O-069. such a constraint does **not** merge the properties **or** authorize deriving classification from a legacy filename alone.
- determine the Claim's contribution under its governing classification authority **and** preserve the separate coordinates **in** its Carrier.
