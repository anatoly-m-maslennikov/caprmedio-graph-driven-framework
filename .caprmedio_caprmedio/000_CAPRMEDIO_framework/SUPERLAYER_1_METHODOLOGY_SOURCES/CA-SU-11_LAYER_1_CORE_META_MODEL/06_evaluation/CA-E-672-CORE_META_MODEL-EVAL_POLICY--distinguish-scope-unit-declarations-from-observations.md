## Summary

Distinguish Scope Unit Declarations from observations

## Claim

An Evaluation fails when a consumer treats an observed folder or Goal as a Project Scope Unit Declaration or a non-Project Scope Delegation, conceals a declared Scope Unit because its Carrier or Goal is absent, or repairs contradictory sources without authorization. A missing Carrier or Goal does not erase its structural Atom or authorize a mutation.
