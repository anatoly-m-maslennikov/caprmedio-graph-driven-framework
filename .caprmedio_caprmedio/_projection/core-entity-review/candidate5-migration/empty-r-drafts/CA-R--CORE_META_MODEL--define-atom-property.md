## Details

Review disposition: superseded aggregate placeholder; do not admit. Scope and Claim remain empty. The file and its historical Subject are retained for review traceability, not as a proposed extra property.

CA-R-1193 defines Property as a dependent characteristic of its bearer. CA-R-1598 requires Atoms to carry their applicable Properties and leaves required, optional and inapplicable fields to their governing model. Neither defines an additional field named Atom.Property.

The Operator clarified that metadata fields and Markdown content sections both represent properties. Candidate #6 decision 29 retains concrete properties such as Atom.Status and Atom.Semantics, connected to their bearer by IS_BORNE_BY. Their in-file representation is a Delivery concern. There is no additional Atom.Property field containing them all. Shared Property meaning remains in the Terms view under decision 3; existing source rules remain unchanged.
