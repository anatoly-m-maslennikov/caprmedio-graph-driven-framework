Match captured source bytes and the selected Core pin. Summary, filename, Subjects, frontmatter and repeated-label incidence alone do not prove meaning. Consolidation, generalization and drop marks need exact evidence, proposed replacements and explicit effects on Relations, constraints, historical references and queries, plus preserved and potentially lost distinctions. A requested mark is a proposal, never adoption. A generalization does not admit a new native subtype. Lack of a checked definition is not proof of emptiness or permission to drop; repeated labels are not duplication proof.

A confirmed relation kind requires its own direction, endpoints, graph ownership and evidence; display nesting or a section label does not create one.
