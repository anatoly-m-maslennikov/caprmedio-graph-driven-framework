# Summary

Require an irreducible ownership boundary for new structural levels

## Scope

admission of a proposed Structural Level.

## Claim

admission of a proposed Structural Level requires identification of **`>=1`** required ownership boundary that **none** of the existing Structural Levels **or** Relations can represent **without** violating single ownership, acyclicity, **or** semantic irreducibility.
