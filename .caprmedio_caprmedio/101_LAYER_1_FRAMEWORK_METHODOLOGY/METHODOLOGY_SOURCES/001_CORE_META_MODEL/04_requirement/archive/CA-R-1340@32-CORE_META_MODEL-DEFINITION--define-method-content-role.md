## Summary

Define Method Content Role

## Claim scope

Atom Claims about how to achieve required outcomes, including architecture, coding policies, engineering decisions and authorship or construction conventions.

## Claim

A Method Atom is an Atom with Content Role Method. Its Claim specifies how required outcomes are achieved, including architecture, coding policies, engineering decisions and authorship or construction conventions, without defining a particular operational Action or Workflow. Common Atom meaning, properties and constraints are inherited.

## Details

A mandatory architecture choice remains Method; being mandatory does not turn a means into a required runtime result. Subject and Content Role are independent: a Method can govern a Domain, Engineering or global Graph Subject.
