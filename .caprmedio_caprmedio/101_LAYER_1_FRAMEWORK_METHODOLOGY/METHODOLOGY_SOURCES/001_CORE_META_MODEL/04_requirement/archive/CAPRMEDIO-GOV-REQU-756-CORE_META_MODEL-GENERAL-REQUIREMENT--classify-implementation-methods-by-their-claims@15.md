an Implementation Method qualified under CAPRMEDIO-META-REQU-744 **must** receive its Local Tier from its complete Claim under the applicable Local Tier definitions **and** classification Method; reusability alone **must not** force Core **or** prevent a concrete reusable realization procedure from occupying Standard.
