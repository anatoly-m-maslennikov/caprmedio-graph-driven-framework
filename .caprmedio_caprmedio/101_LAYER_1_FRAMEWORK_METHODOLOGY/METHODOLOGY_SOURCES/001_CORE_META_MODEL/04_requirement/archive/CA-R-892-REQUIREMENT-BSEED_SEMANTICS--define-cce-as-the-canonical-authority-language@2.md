# Define CCE as the canonical authority language

CAPRMEDIO Controlled English (CCE) must be the canonical formal language for every authority-bearing Bootstrap Seed Claim and Principle Claim. Every CCE Claim must have exactly one interpretation under one identified CCE version whose registered grammar, vocabulary, statement forms, predicate signatures, quantifiers, modalities, negation rules, and applicability rules determine that interpretation; unsupported, unknown, ill-typed, or ambiguous language must not establish authority.

Human-readable explanations and filename summaries must be non-authoritative Projections derived from the canonical CCE Claim. A Projection must not add, remove, or change the Claim's identity, bearer, modality, polarity, predicate, participants, quantity, Applicability, or other action-guiding meaning.
