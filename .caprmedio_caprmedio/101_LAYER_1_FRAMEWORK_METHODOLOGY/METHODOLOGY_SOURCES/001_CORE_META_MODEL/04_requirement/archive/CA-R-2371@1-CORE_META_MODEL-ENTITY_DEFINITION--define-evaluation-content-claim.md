Define Evaluation Content Claim

## Claim scope

The primary Evaluation contribution carried by an Evaluation Atom.

## Claim

Evaluation Content Claim is the one direct prose Claim carrying one complete scoped Evaluation contribution: a falsifiable check, acceptance criterion or disposition rule against identified authority and Scope, with reproducible inputs, evidence, expected observations, acceptance policy and disposition.
