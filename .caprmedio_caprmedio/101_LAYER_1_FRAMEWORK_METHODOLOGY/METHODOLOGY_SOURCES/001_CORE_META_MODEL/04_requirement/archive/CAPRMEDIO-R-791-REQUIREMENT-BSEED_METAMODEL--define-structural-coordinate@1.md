# Define Structural coordinate

A Structural coordinate is a derived locator comprising a Structural unit's `structural_level` and, only for an `ordered_unit`, its `local_order`; an ordered coordinate is rendered `structural_level/local_order`. It locates the unit within the structural model without replacing its stable identity or Structural path.
