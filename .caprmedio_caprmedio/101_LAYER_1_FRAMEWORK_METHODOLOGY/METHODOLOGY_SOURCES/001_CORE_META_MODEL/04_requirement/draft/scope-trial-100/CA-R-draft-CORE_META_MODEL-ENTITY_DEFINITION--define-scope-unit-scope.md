## Summary

Define a Scope Unit's purpose, responsibilities, results and boundaries

## Claim

A Scope Unit's Scope describes its coherent purpose, responsibilities, expected results and boundaries. A Requirement of Type Scope Definition expresses that Scope for its explicit Target Scope Unit. Related responsibilities and conditions may remain in that single definition when one concise Summary faithfully covers their whole meaning.

## Semantics

Scope is the unit's responsibility contract, not its filesystem address. The textual Scope is expressed by the defining Requirement; this proposal does not require a duplicate Scope value in governing configuration. Specific outcome constraints may remain additional Requirements.
