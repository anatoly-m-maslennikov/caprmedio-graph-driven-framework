# Summary

Classify Terms Projection under Projection

## Decision

Projection is a direct narrower kind of Continuant. Terms Projection is a narrower kind of Projection: Continuant/Projection/Terms Projection. It is not a replacement for Term. This retains Term from the Operator's stated tree and adds the requested Projection specialization.

The classification prepares a Definition Draft only. It neither creates a Terms Projection artifact nor starts the deferred Terms Graph implementation/review. Terms Projection is derived from its declared sources and does not become a new authoritative layer of Term definitions.
