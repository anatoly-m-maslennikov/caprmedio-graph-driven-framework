## Summary

Evaluate craft-independent project operation

## Claim

## Claim checked

the Operator can complete a representative Project lifecycle through direction **and** control **without** personally performing specialist craft work.

## Applicable conditions

apply **to** an Operator-accepted representative Project lifecycle within applicable authority **and** feasibility constraints.

## Acceptance

pass **only** **when** the Operator can direct, deliver, run, **and** maintain the representative Project **without** acquiring **or** personally performing its specialist implementation work.

## Failure

report **every** lifecycle stage that requires the Operator **to** acquire **or** personally perform specialist implementation work.
