## Summary

Define Property

## Claim

### Prose Claim

Property means a Node Property in `Node.**` whose direct `IS_PROPERTY_OF` Relation identifies exactly one immediate bearer, which may be a Node or Node Property. The bearer-qualified Subject is written `Bearer.Property`; `HAS_PROPERTY` is the derived inverse view.

This Claim defines the reusable lexical Term `Property` at `Term//Property`.
