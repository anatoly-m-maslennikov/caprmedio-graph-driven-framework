## Summary

Validate implementation retry-limit resolution and accounting

## Claim

### Prose Claim

Validate retry-limit resolution and accounting for a Workflow under its applicable Workflow specification and governing permissions. This Evaluation does not declare a retry Property or choose a source-precedence policy.

- Check a valid applicable allowance, including zero; a missing, invalid, or ambiguous allowance stops the affected automatic retry and requests Operator disposition. Do not infer an allowance or bypass a confidence, approval, or other Operator constraint.
- The initial failed Evaluation consumes no retry. Each permitted fix-and-evaluate round consumes one retry when it starts; repeated permission checks do not consume another.
- Preserve consumed accounting for the same Workflow execution when the failure set changes or the next loop begins. Exhaustion stops another retry and reports remaining failures; success does not start an additional repair retry.
- Preserve the selected allowance and its evidence without changing source values or granting additional authority. Earlier escalation required by authority remains valid even if allowance remains.

Exercise these cases against an actual governed Workflow and its declared allowance source when that Workflow is available; this case does not create that Workflow or prescribe its Carrier.
