## Summary

Declare Step

## Claim

### Formal Claim

```CFEL
# model
Atom//(.Header.Role := Operations)//Step;

Atom//(.Header.Role := Operations)//Step >| (Container == yes);

# instances
$step[<Step>];

$step{::1}((IS_CONTAINED_BY)) $parent[<Workflow> OR <Step>];

IF $step {=0}((RUNS)) THEN $step {::1+}((CONTAINS)) $nested_step[<Step>];

IF $step {>0}((RUNS)) THEN $step {::0}((CONTAINS));

IF $step {=0}((CONTAINS)) THEN $step {::1}((RUNS)) $selected[<Action> OR <Workflow>];

$definition[<Step>] {::0+}((HAS_EXECUTION)) $run[<Step Run>]
```

### Prose Claim

Every Step’s Container chain ends at a Workflow.

## Semantics

A Step prescribes work within a Workflow.
