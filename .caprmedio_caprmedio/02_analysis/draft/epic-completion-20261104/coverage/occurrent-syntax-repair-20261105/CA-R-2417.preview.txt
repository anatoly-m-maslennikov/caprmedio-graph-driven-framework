## Summary

Define HAS_EXECUTION Relation

## Claim

### Formal Claim

```CFEL
# model
$relation[<Relation>, (.Name = HAS_EXECUTION)];

$inverse1[<Relation>, (.Name = IS_EXECUTION_OF)];

$relation <((HAS_INVERSE)) $inverse1;

$relation ((HAS_CLASSIFICATION)) Derivation == Derived;

# instances
$workflow[<Workflow>] ((HAS_EXECUTION)) $workflow_run[<Workflow Run>];

$step[<Step>] ((HAS_EXECUTION)) $step_run[<Step Run>];

$action[<Action>] ((HAS_EXECUTION)) $action_run[<Action Run>]
```

## Semantics

HAS_EXECUTION relates a reusable Workflow, Step or Action to its corresponding actual Run.
