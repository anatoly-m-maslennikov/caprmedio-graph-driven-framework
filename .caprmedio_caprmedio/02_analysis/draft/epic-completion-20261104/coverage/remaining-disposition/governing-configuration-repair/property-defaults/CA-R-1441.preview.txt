## Summary

Define Default Settings

## Claim

### Prose Claim

Default Settings are baseline values for applicable Project.Settings parameters, declared by their corresponding Property Declarations. A default follows the Property’s declared Format and allowed domain. Defaults are distinct from Project initialization inputs and Operator-selected values.
