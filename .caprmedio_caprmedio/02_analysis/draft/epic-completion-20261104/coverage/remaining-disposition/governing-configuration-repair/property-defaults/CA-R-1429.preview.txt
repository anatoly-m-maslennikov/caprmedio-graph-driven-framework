## Summary

Separate settings boundaries from field specifications

## Claim

### Prose Claim

Project.Settings foundations must define their purpose, content and authority boundaries. An independently governable shared settings specification must preserve those foundations without selecting a concrete representation. Each concrete section, field or Carrier syntax specification must be explicit.

A setting does not require a shared settings specification unless it needs independent shared authority.
