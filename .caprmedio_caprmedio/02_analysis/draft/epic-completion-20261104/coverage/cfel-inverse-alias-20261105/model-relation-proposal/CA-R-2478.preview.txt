## Summary

Define composition and its inverse

## Claim

### Formal Claim

```CFEL
# model
$part ((IS_CONTAINED_BY)) $container;

Relation//CONTAINS ((IS_INVERSE_ALIAS_FOR)) Relation//IS_CONTAINED_BY;

alias(Relation//IS_CONTAINED_BY) == "+>";

alias(Relation//CONTAINS) == "/";

Relation//IS_CONTAINED_BY ((HAS_CLASSIFICATION)) Derivation == Direct
```

### Prose Claim

IS_CONTAINED_BY relates a contained declared Entity or instance to its immediate Container. The Target has classification `Container = yes`. Containment does not imply narrowing or inheritance. CONTAINS is its inverse alias: its written Host and Target are the canonical Target and Host, respectively. Both writings identify the same fact. `Whole/Part` writes the inverse CONTAINS view; `Part +> Whole` writes the canonical direction.
