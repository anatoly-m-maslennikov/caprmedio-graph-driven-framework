## Summary

Qualify Allowed Values in Subject Expressions

## Claim

### Prose Claim


allowed-value qualification in a Subject Expression.


**in** a Subject Expression, `X := Y` expresses the HAS_ALLOWED_VALUE Relation from X to Y in X's qualified context, irrespective of X's kind or path. It does not assign Y to an occurrence or determine direct-value cardinality. In a qualified Subject selection such as `X//(.p := Y)`, retain `:=` for the allowed-value qualification and selection; the applicable authority must admit Y.
