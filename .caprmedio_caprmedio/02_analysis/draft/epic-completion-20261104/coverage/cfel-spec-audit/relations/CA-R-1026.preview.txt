## Summary

Register depends_on and required_by relation pair

## Claim

### Formal Claim

```CFEL
# model
Relation//depends_on ((HAS_INVERSE)) Relation//required_by;

Relation//depends_on ((HAS_CLASSIFICATION)) Derivation == Direct;

# instances
$dependent ((depends_on)) $prerequisite
```

### Prose Claim

`depends_on` is a direct dependency-ordering Relation from a dependent Artifact to its prerequisite Artifact. It does not express Plan start dependencies.
