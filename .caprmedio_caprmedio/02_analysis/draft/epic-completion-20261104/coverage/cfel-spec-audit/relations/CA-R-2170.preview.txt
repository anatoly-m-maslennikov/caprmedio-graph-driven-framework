## Summary

Define is derived relation of relation

## Claim

### Formal Claim

```CFEL
# model
Relation//$relation ((IS_DERIVED_RELATION_OF)) Relation//$relation_2;

Relation//IS_DERIVED_RELATION_OF ((HAS_CLASSIFICATION)) Derivation == Direct
```

### Prose Claim

IS_DERIVED_RELATION_OF relates a derived Relation Type instance to its source Relation Type instance. It does not itself specify the endpoint transformation or any qualification. A direct Declaration's inverse binding entails the bound inverse's IS_DERIVED_RELATION_OF link to the direct Relation. This inferred metadata is not repeated in the minimal derived Declaration. A qualified derivation must retain its explicit qualification; it must not be normalized to an unconditional inverse. This Relation is distinct from DERIVED_FROM between represented-content versions.
