## Summary

Register depends_on and required_by relation pair

## Claim

### Formal Claim

```CFEL
# model
Relation//required_by ((IS_INVERSE_ALIAS_FOR)) Relation//depends_on;

Relation//depends_on ((HAS_CLASSIFICATION)) Derivation == Direct;

$host ((depends_on)) $target
```

### Prose Claim

#### Claim scope
the `depends_on` **and** `required_by` relation pair.

`depends_on` **means** a direct dependency-ordering relation from a dependent Artifact **to** its prerequisite Artifact, with `required_by` as its inverse alias. Plan start dependencies use the Plan Graph's `BLOCKS` under CA-R-2762 instead; this Relation Kind **must not** duplicate that scheduling fact.

Both endpoints are Artifacts: the Host is dependent and the Target is its prerequisite.
Forward `Host ((depends_on)) Target` and reverse `Target <((depends_on)) Host` are views of the same Relation fact.
depends_on relates dependent Artifact to prerequisite Artifact.
