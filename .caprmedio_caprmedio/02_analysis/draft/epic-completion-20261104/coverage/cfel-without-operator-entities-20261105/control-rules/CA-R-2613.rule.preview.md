## Summary

Define generic Relation Declaration endpoint signature

## Claim

### Prose Claim

Within a Relation Declaration Formal Claim only, `$host ((RELATION)) $target` is the reusable generic endpoint signature. `$host` and `$target` range over declared model entities or instances, including Carrier and CFEL, and exclude literals. This signature creates neither a Relation fact nor instance data. Known endpoint kinds remain typed selectors; the generic signature does not extend untyped variables to any other CFEL context.
