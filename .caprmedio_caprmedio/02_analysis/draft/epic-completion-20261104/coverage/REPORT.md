# MODEL Subject coverage audit — review packet

The input is the published seven-projection audit snapshot pinned in `manifest.json`, derived from its 1,151-source frontier. `findings.tsv` records all 64 missing-coverage flags and 26 unresolved expressions exactly once, with occurrence paths, source hashes and review dispositions. Dispositions identify the evidence and next decision, not proof that a proposed migration is correct. The live source pins must be checked again at adoption.

Three full-source `.preview.txt` files propose Subject-only repairs: CA-R-1014 and CA-E-403 replace the obsolete `Atom.Header.Subjects.Governs` dependency with the declared `Atom.Header.Subjects.Active`; CA-R-2690 qualifies its Delivery Atom dependency under the explicit MODEL namespace and declared `Header.Role`. The manifest pins each predecessor, proposed bytes and relevant declarations. The previews change no body or non-Subject metadata. They are proposals, not Current edits.

Three legacy Term addresses have exact registered Term Definitions (Artifact CA-M-454, ID CA-M-450, Property CA-M-453). That demonstrates registration of the direct Terms, not automatic equivalence of the old hierarchical paths. `Term//Type` and the legacy Dependent Entity term have no matching Term Definition in the selected frontier. The reader now supports exact registered Terms as informational, but this snapshot predates rebuilding with that fix.

The MODEL union `(Node,Node.**)` is a legitimate complex selection scope, and the bare MODEL namespace is a scope, not an Entity. Relation chains and historical cardinality spellings retain their authored scope and need separate grammar or target review. Seventeen Projection-related flags are routed to the separately owned Projection lane, including the malformed YAML mapping in CA-E-672. Names lacking a namespace are not automatically MODEL, and an instance selection is not admitted as a model kind.

Framework Instance Settings and Project Settings are explicitly distinct in CA-E-671; the 16 legacy settings addresses therefore cannot be mechanically rewritten solely because some suffixes match Project.Settings declarations. Other legacy hierarchy flags and nine exact-declaration-not-found flags remain review candidates, not proven authority defects. Preserve original semantic scope and create a question where evidence is insufficient.

Acceptance before any application: verify all source, declaration and frontier hashes against live bytes; review the three proposed exact subject changes against their Claims and governing declarations; ensure no duplicate passive values are introduced; validate affected carriers with the repository's governing tools; archive exact predecessors and promote only under the separately authorized workflow; rebuild all seven projections from the updated pinned frontier; compare both coverage and unresolved lists, preserve source provenance, and inspect every remaining item rather than assuming a zero count. This packet does not apply source changes, regenerate projections, establish runtime behavior, or close the Epic.
