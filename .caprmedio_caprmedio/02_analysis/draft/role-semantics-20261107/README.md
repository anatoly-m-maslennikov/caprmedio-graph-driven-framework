# Role Semantics updates

Four successor Drafts and one updated Operations Draft. All five Current Declarations remain unchanged.

| Defined Role | Draft | Semantics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requirement | CA-R-2512@3-draft | A Requirement states a required outcome, behavior, property or constraint, or defines a governed model and its constraints. It applies across Aspects, including domain behavior, engineering performance, evaluation effectiveness and delivery reliability. |
| Method | CA-R-2513@3-draft | A Method specifies domain and engineering designs, mechanisms or working approaches for achieving required results. |
| Evaluation | CA-R-2514@3-draft | An Evaluation specifies falsifiable checks and how their evidence is interpreted to determine whether required results are achieved. |
| Delivery | CA-R-2515@3-draft | A Delivery specifies how results are represented, packaged, stored and made available through their Carriers at their intended destinations. |
| Operations | CA-R-2463@3-draft | An Operations Atom specifies work to perform and how it is coordinated, including its sequence, inputs and outputs. |
