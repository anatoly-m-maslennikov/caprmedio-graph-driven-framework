- Inputs: the exact selected question/proposal and alternative versions; the bounded independent-choice list supplied by the caller; literal Operator input/annotation with actual provenance and prospective scope; existing settled decisions/delegation; current applicable Goal/Principles/authority, effective confidence and relevant current source state. If the calling domain requires a record or other prerequisite that is absent, return that absence; do not fabricate it or impose Journal recording on every generic question.
