# Evaluate semantic mapping admissibility

## Claim checked

An Extension or Project Adaptation mapping is safe to rely on within its
declared scope.

## Applicable conditions

Apply before activating a mapping or relying on it for governed work, and
again after its source, canonical target, rule, scope, or required canonical
distinction changes.

## Check

Verify that the source element, exact canonical target, mapping rule, intended
scope, and required canonical distinctions are declared. Verify that the
target is the sole canonical owner under CA-M-105. Verify either that the
mapping preserves every required distinction in scope or that the Operator has
accepted its specific specialization or loss and consequences.

## Acceptance

Pass only when all declared fields are present, canonical ownership is unique,
and preservation or the accepted bounded exception is evidenced.

## Failure and stop

Fail on a missing field, competing canonical owner, unproven required
distinction, or unaccepted specialization or loss. Stop the affected mapping
and adaptation; return the evidence and unresolved choices to the Operator.
