# Deliver brownfield Inventory and adoption Tools

WHEN CA-P-902 is Done, THE Assignee MUST deliver the installed `IMPLEMENTATION_INVENTORY` Finder and `ADOPT_RECONCILE` Doer for explicit `full`, `continue`, and `partial` brownfield adoption through CRMED drafts only.

## Scope

`(Atom ID IN (CA-R-1071, CA-R-1072, CA-M-101, CA-M-102, CA-E-291, CA-E-292))`

## Definition of Done

THE Task is NOT DONE IF (CA-P-902 is not Done OR the Inventory cannot reproducibly record one exact selected native frontier of directories, files, language-native modules, declared dependencies, and detectable realization relations without Analysis or authority OR adoption cannot distinguish full, continue, and partial frontiers OR any adoption mode silently rewrites active authority or emits an active CRMED Atom OR the structural step treats the folder and file heuristic as observed fact instead of an explicit draft-producing interpretation OR unresolved ambiguity is discarded instead of preserved as a Concern draft or reviewable finding OR Step 1 cannot remain independently useful without Step 2 OR repeated unchanged execution is not idempotent OR fresh installed tests do not prove currentness, complete selected-target coverage, draft-only output, singular and bulk operation, and safe failure without native-code mutation).

## Details

Keep Inventory generation mutation-free. The adoption Doer consumes an exact current Inventory and may propose Area drafts for folders containing folders, Feature drafts for folders containing files, and Module drafts from language-native modules or a source-file fallback. Every classification records its heuristic and ambiguity boundary. The operator separately promotes any accepted draft through the canonical MCP mutation gateway.
