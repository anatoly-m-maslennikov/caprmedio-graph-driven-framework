# Prioritize human comprehension and decisive structure

CAPRMEDIO presents the smallest decisive structure that lets an intended reader
understand, review, and use the governed meaning without first learning
incidental implementation machinery or decorative formalism.

Additional terminology, metadata, hierarchy, notation, and ceremony are
admitted only when they expose a distinction, boundary, obligation, or action
that the intended reader materially needs. Machine convenience must not make
the canonical human-governed surface harder to interpret. Formal detail may be
added progressively when the receiving use requires it, while the plain
meaning remains available.
