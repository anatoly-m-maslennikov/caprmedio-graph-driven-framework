# Model project structure as numbered levels

CAPRMEDIO **must** model the caprmedio Scope Unit at Structural Level **`0`** **and** its descendant Scope Units at positive Structural Levels derived from their ancestry; a Structural Level groups peer positions but does **not** itself establish parentage, order, **or** authority.
