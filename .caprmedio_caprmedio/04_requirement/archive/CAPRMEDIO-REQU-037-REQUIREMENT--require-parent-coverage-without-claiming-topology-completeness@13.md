# Require parent coverage without claiming topology completeness

In strict authority mode, every active tier-classified PRMEDO Atom must have at least one permitted active parent unless the applicable Type authority registers its Type as orphan-permitted. A permitted parent is the active Intent Atom for a Project Principle, an applicable lower-global-tier Atom in the same structural scope, or an Atom permitted by the global tier topology in an ancestor structural scope. This parent-coverage condition is necessary but insufficient for authority-topology completeness and cannot establish Principle-set completeness.
