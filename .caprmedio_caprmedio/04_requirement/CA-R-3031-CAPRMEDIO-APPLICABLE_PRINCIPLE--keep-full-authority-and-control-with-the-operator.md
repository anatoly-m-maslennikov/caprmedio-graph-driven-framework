## Summary

Keep full authority and control with the Operator

## Claim

### Prose Claim

The Operator has the final say over the Project and its CAPRMEDIO instance. The Operator can delegate authority to AI Agents, change it, or withdraw it, and can observe, guide, stop, and recover important work.
