# Screen short names for unintended readings

**before** admitting a short name, abbreviation, **or** prefix, inspect its case-insensitive tokens **and** joined form for unintended obscene, insulting, deceptive, **or** distracting readings **in** the Operator's languages; reject candidates such as `ANAL`, `BS`, **and** `METH`, **and** choose the shortest readable alternative that preserves the canonical meaning.
