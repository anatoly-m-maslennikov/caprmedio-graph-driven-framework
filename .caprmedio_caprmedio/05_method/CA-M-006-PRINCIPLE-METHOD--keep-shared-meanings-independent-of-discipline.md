# Keep shared meanings independent of discipline

CAPRMEDIO must keep its shared canonical meanings independent of discipline.

## Formal statement

Let \(C\) be the set of shared canonical meanings and \(D\) the set of disciplines.

\[
\forall c\in C,\forall d_1,d_2\in D:\operatorname{Meaning}(c,d_1)=\operatorname{Meaning}(c,d_2)
\]
