# Maintain one canonical semantic model

For each shared CAPRMEDIO meaning, maintain exactly one canonical owner. Treat
other representations as mappings, explanations, or realizations rather than
additional canonical definitions. When competing canonical owners cannot be
resolved without a meaning decision, stop the affected mapping or adaptation
and return the alternatives and their evidence to the Operator.
