# Screen short names for unintended readings

**before** admitting a short name, abbreviation, **or** prefix, inspect its case-insensitive tokens **and** joined form for unintended obscene, insulting, deceptive, **or** distracting readings **in** the Operator's languages. **if** such a reading is found, **then** present a concise warning that explains the reading **and** offers readable alternatives preserving the canonical meaning, preferring the shortest suitable alternative. do **not** automatically reject the candidate on that basis; allow the Operator **to** accept the warned name explicitly.
