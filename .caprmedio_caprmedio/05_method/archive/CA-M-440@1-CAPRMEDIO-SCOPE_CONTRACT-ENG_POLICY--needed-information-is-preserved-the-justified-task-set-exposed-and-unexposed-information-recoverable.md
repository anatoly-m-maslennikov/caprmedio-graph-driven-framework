## Summary

Needed information is preserved, the justified task set exposed, and unexposed information recoverable

## Claim scope

exposing information for a task.

## Claim

**to** expose information for a task:

- preserve **all** information necessary for governed use;
- expose from the preserved information the complete currently justified sufficient information set for that task **and** nothing outside that set; **and**
- keep **all** unexposed preserved information recoverable.
