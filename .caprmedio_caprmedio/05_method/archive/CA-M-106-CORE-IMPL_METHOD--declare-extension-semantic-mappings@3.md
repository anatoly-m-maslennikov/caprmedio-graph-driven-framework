# Declare Extension semantic mappings

Before an Extension relies on an adapted shared element, declare its source
element, exact canonical target, mapping rule, intended scope, and the
canonical distinctions that the Extension must preserve. Submit the mapping to
the admissibility evaluation in CA-E-249. Do not activate or rely on a mapping
whose target or preservation boundary is unresolved.
