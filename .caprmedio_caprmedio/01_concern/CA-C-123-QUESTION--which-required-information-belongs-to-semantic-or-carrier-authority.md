# Which required information belongs to semantic or Carrier authority?

which parts of CA-R-1037, CA-R-1220, **and** META-REQU-097 define required information independently of a Carrier, **and** which parts specify what a Carrier stores **and** therefore belong **in** Delivery authority?

## Evidence

- CA-R-1037 requires a Lineage Impact Analysis **to** identify exact changed Revisions, classification basis, responsibility, reviewed children, outcomes, recursive results, **and** its conclusion. it does **not** name concrete keys, placement, **or** a document template.
- CA-R-1220 requires an Installed Extensions Catalog Entry **to** identify **=1** immutable candidate by identity, Version, Author, Source, Source Version, Origin, **and** Content Digest. it does **not** select the catalog's concrete field encoding.
- CA-R-1339 admits required logical characteristics **and** outcomes under Requirement. CA-R-1342 places stored content, fields, formats, **and** Carrier representation under Delivery. a list of information alone does **not** establish which responsibility owns these two existing Claims.

- META-REQU-097 also requires relied-on Evidence **to** identify its supported Claim, relevant Carrier **or** Journal Record, material producing **or** interpreting work **or** Method, **and** applicable scope **and** time boundary. these are logical binding obligations **without** named Carrier keys; the provenance-versus-proof distinction **must** survive **any** separation.

## Principle check

CA-M-002 rejects duplicating the same obligation **in** R **and** D merely **to** satisfy a role screen. CA-M-006 requires a coherent semantic/representation boundary; CA-M-005 rejects invented templates **or** Properties; CA-R-1490 requires preservation of the specified information **and** provenance. these Principles do **not** choose an absent Carrier contract **or** justify dropping required information.

## Disposition

defer these role changes under the Operator's uncertainty policy. preserve their current Claims **and** exact identities. establish the intended semantic information obligation **and** concrete Carrier responsibility from accepted authority, **then** replace **or** split **only** the independently owned responsibilities **without** weakening provenance **or** copying the same obligation into multiple owners. this Concern does **not** declare the present role correct **or** authorize new file formats.
