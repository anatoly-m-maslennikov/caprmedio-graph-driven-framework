# How should the PROGRAMMATIC source ratchet be partitioned without losing conditions?

which independently governed conventions **and** Carrier choices should replace the combined M-162 source ratchet?

## Evidence

M-162 combines file-size **and** executable-unit-size limits, complexity checking, bounded exceptions, static-data externalization, concrete format choices, **and** admission behavior. preserving it as **=1** Claim merely because **all** clauses concern source quality would not establish their inseparability. splitting by numbered item alone would also ignore shared exceptions **and** acceptance conditions.

## Principle check

coherence requires matching claims **and** checked targets; DRY preserves reuse of M-157/M-158/M-160 instead of repeating their rules. minimum complexity rejects unnecessary Entities **or** Actions. information preservation requires retaining the 200/25/26–40/40/20/25 limits, their units, applicability, exception gates, **and** the distinction between navigation constraints **and** quality proof.

## Disposition

preserve M-162 while mapping each independently replaceable convention **and** its consumers. keep Implementation choices **in** M, concrete Carrier encoding **in** D, **and** any independent admission Action **in** O. do **not** invent Settings parameters, change numeric limits, remove exceptions, **or** claim conformance from a size score. the ordinary-opening rendering repair does **not** settle this partition.
