# Summary

What replaces the generic Entity topic in Plans?

## Question

Should a generic old Entity tag select a specific Node, Projection or Carrier when the Plan does not establish that narrower meaning?

## Scope

Exact standalone Entity tags in Current Plans only. Qualified Entity/... expressions and every RMEDO owner remain separately reviewed.

## Details

Bounded choice: use the existing generic Subject root. Candidate #7 explicitly replaces the old single Entity-root proposal with Subject. Do not narrow a vague root tag to Node or Projection just because the body mentions a graph or view.

This retains the tag's broad topic character without asserting that every represented thing is a Node, every graph is a Projection instance, or every topic has a Carrier. The task's actual Objective and scope remain unchanged; Subject is topic indexing, not a grant of universal authority.

The read-only lane112 keyword candidates are not admitted source mappings. In particular, incidental mention of a Projection in Journal-authority work is not evidence that the old Entity tag means Projection. No new Domain entity or property is created.

## Operator clarification — 2026-10-24

Remove the Atom.Entity property. Preserve its useful topic meaning in Subjects
and its exact applicability boundary in Content Scope, replacing the older
Substance Scope name. Do not create a replacement Atom.Entity property.

That answers the property question, not every generic Entity tag in the old
Plans. Do not infer a blanket Node, Projection, Carrier or Subject replacement
from it. This Question stays Active for the remaining source-specific tags;
it is not a reason to re-ask the settled Atom.Entity decision. No generic
Entity-tag mapping is approved by this clarification alone.

## Resolution

Fresh Current inventory311 contains no standalone Entity Subject tag in any
of its 1,721 Plans. The bounded Plan-tag question therefore has no remaining
instance to decide. This does not approve a generic replacement for every
qualified Entity expression or for the remaining Method dependency named Entity.

The Operator's separate property decision remains: remove Atom.Entity,
preserve useful topics in Subjects and exact applicability in Content Scope.
That decision is not evidence that every defining rule or content consumer
has already migrated. No new source mapping is applied by closing this record.
