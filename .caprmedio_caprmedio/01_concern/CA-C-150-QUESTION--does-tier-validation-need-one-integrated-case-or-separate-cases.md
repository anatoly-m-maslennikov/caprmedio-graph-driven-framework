# Does tier validation need one integrated case or separate cases?

which criteria **in** E-447 are necessary parts of one integrated tier-boundary case, **and** which are independently replaceable checks that require separate Evaluation Atoms?

## Evidence

E-447 combines semantic tier classification, source-target groupings, Settings counterexamples, Global Tier arithmetic, Atom selections, expansion boundaries, filename encoding, **and** mixed-Claim admission. its old fixed count of twelve E-446 targets is repaired **to** follow **all** current targets, **and** its acceptance clauses are structured. neither repair proves a one-Claim boundary. some criteria exercise interactions among tiers; others also have their own authority **and** individual Evaluations. the failure-disposition paragraph additionally names restoration **and** rerun behavior.

## Principle check

CA-M-002 rejects duplicate test authority; CA-M-005 rejects splitting solely because the fixture is long; CA-M-006 requires compatible local **and** integrated checks; CA-R-1490 protects concrete counterexamples **and** failure restrictions. composite Claims are allowed, but grouping unrelated obligations under one title does **not** prove one Claim.

## Disposition

preserve **every** existing fixture **and** expected result. identify the independent acceptance predicate **and** exact checked authority for **every** case **before** deciding to retain, reference, split, **or** deduplicate it. preserve integrated cases needed **to** detect cross-rule failures. any extraction of restoration **or** rerun behavior **must** reuse admitted Operations **and** retain its authorization boundaries; an Evaluation instruction does **not** grant mutation permission.
