# Summary

Can legacy Plan Artifact topics remain Terms?

## Question

Can the standalone Artifact Subject in P1090 and P1091 use the existing
Term//Artifact topic while their unresolved qualified aliases stay unchanged?

## Scope

Two Current Plans. Only the exact standalone Artifact Subject changes.
No task body, carrier policy, taxonomy or lifecycle changes.

## Details

Bounded choice: yes. P1090 explicitly discusses a Projection's own Artifact
identity, and P1091 discusses one Artifact represented in multiple graphs.
The Operator made Artifact a Term, not an object branch. Current R1424 and
Candidate #7 already use that Term target. This is not a generic Term fallback
for unknown nouns and does not endorse the older task text as a new model rule.

Keep Artifact/Carrier, Artifact/Revision and Entity literal until their own
source-specific review. Do not turn Artifact into a bearer for invented
properties or restore the obsolete Revision entity. The source proof records
the small uncertainty, body anchors and exact old/new Subject sets.
