# Shorter Summary trial

The seven role writers were tested on fifteen small cases. Each invocation wrote the full content, including Summary, with its internal check. No source Atom was edited.

## Results

- Summary words: **111 → 85**, a **23.4% reduction**.
- Nine Summaries became shorter by word count, five stayed the same, and one became longer.
- Independent review accepted **15/15 Summaries and 15/15 bodies** in the final sample.
- The longer R2 Summary states the Version Number definition; its older title only said to define the property.

The prompts now tell the writer to remove filler, repeated scope and supporting details from Summary while keeping its main meaning and distinctive context. Exact terms and names remain unchanged. Full conditions remain in the body; a short Summary must not contradict them.

## Evidence and limits

This was an agent-based development trial, not a blind evaluation or a production reliability estimate. The first pass accepted fourteen Summaries and all fifteen bodies; E2 became too generic. P, M and E were then refined and their six cases freshly generated from source facts. The other nine cases were unchanged and their verdicts carried forward after hash checks. Outputs were not manually fixed.

Each sampled final output came from one invocation of its role writer, without external review findings as input. This does not mean the prompts themselves were designed in one pass. Several cases resemble in-prompt examples. Large documents, every specialized Type, and arbitrary incomplete specifications were not tested. A remains outside the fixed-template set.
