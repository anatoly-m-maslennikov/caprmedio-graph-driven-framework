## Summary

Trial Scope Definitions and Summary coherence on one hundred Atoms

## Objective

Test the proposed Scope Definition and practical Atom-coherence model on exactly 100 Current source Atoms, including every Current Goal. Use Terra at high effort; produce reviewed Draft proposals and report where the approach fits badly before deciding broader adoption.

## Details

The complete Current Goal scan finds 47 Goals, including the Operator-owned Project Goal at the source root. The earlier role-folder placement census did not include that special root carrier; this trial explicitly includes it. Add 53 non-Goal RMEDO sources with varied roles, Methodology/Engine/project ownership and Summary lengths. Exclude Drafts, archives, installed copies and Projections. All existing Atoms remain unchanged.

Try R/Scope Definition in place of Goal: the Scope is the Scope Unit's purpose, responsibility, expected results and boundary, not merely a container address. Prefer one coherent Scope Definition per resolved target Scope Unit; consolidate related standing Goals rather than relabel each as a separate definition. Preserve specific outcome requirements as additional Rs and finite work as P Objectives where that is a better fit. Do not discard meaning or force unrelated obligations into a vague summary. Record unresolved targets/owners and unregistered scopes rather than inventing structure. Project root keeps its existing Operator ownership in the candidate until the template's admission rules are reviewed.

The practical Atom boundary is its whole meaning: one concise Summary must cover the content. This is not a single logical predicate per Atom. Related conditions and clauses can stay together. Compare proposed 70- and 100-character Summary limits, including spaces; 100 is the suggested trial ceiling, not an adopted Current rule. A topic label or a laundry list is not a faithful meaning summary. One Claim section may contain several coherent statements.

Use the latest Workflow responsibility split: Core owns reusable business workflow meaning, Engine owns technical realization, Project owns project-specific Local/Public Release orchestration. Project Workflow Steps may call workflows owned by Methodology or Engine; caller/callee retain their owners. No code, runtime, actual moves, live promotions or source archival is included in this trial.

Use four disjoint Terra-high lanes. Each lane reads full selected source bodies, produces scratch candidate Draft payloads and a thin source-to-proposal trace; parent creates actual Draft files and performs integration. Keep every Goal's meaning traceable, including missing-target cases. Do not produce large copied-Claim mappings.

## Definition of Done

Exactly 100 source paths and all 47 Current Goal paths are accounted for. Candidate Drafts and hold/supplement/consolidation decisions are explicit. The report compares 70/100-character Summary fit, preserves source meaning, states before/after document counts and highlights failures instead of treating the new model as proven. No Current Atom is changed or retired.

## Progress

Active — sample pinned; four Terra-high lanes are ready. Broad adoption remains undecided until the trial result is reviewed.
