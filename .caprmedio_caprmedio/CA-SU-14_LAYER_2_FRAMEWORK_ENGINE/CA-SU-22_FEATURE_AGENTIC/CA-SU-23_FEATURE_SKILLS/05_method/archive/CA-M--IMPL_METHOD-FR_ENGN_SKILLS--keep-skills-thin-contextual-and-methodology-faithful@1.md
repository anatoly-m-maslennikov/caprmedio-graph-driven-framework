# Keep Skills thin, contextual, and Methodology-faithful

Use a Skill as the Agent-facing wrapper for applicable FRAMEWORK_METHODOLOGY authority and Engine capabilities. Its short description supports discovery; its body selects and sequences only the context, Methods, Tools, MCP capabilities, and App interactions needed for the current task. Load larger references and assets only when the active procedure needs them.

Keep judgment and operator interaction in the Agent procedure and delegate repeatable mechanics to software capabilities. Do not duplicate canonical Methodology, Tool behavior, schemas, or project truth inside Skill prose. Give every delegation an explicit target, input, authority boundary, expected result, and stop or escalation condition, then evaluate whether the Skill selected the right authority and capabilities rather than merely whether execution completed.

Candidate alignment: CA-M-002, CA-M-005, CA-M-006, CA-D-002, CA-R-861.
