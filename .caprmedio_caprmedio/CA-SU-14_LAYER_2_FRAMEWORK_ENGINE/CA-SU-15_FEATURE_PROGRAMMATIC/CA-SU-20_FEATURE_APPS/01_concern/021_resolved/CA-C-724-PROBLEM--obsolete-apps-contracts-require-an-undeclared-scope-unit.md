## Summary

Obsolete APPS contracts require an undeclared Scope Unit

## Concern

Seven Current topology, registration, evaluation and delivery sources require an AGENT_HOST_PLUGINS child or obsolete structural levels and paths. Current Scope Delegations declare WORKBENCH and GRAPH_UI under APPS; they do not declare that third unit. Implementing the old sources would restore a rejected structure.

## Details

Prepare normal retirement of R1100, R1101, M196, M242, E314, E408 and D489 after survivor acceptance and reference repair. Preserve headless/non-authoritative Workbench and optional Graph UI outcomes in R1603/R1076. Preserve generic host-package nonduplication in the externally owned R1606 Goal targeting existing APPS. Preserve headless acceptance in E673. No new Scope Unit or native plugin is required.

Resolve only after those survivor changes are Current, obsolete sources are archived and current references are repaired. Drafts and a review report alone do not fix this problem.

## Proposed resolution after adoption

Adopt the reviewed survivors CA-R-1603, CA-R-1606, CA-R-2730 and CA-E-673, retire the seven obsolete sources identified above, and repair their Current incoming references. The resulting source disposition preserves the headless Workbench and host-package boundaries without declaring the obsolete child. Resolve this Concern only after those adoption and retirement guards pass; this Draft is not evidence of runtime conformance.
