## Summary

Verify canonical native Tool routing

## Claim

### Prose Claim

A frontier passes canonical routing only when each exposed native Tool delegates to its canonical owner with no invented endpoint or hidden composition. A direct read remains at its canonical Tool without forced Workflow routing, and registration/generic call gating do not require Atom-catalog or source-manifest enumeration.

Verify the inventory of configured code providers and route names. Registration and generic call gating must work without Atom-catalog or source-manifest enumeration, while operation execution still validates its business prerequisites.
