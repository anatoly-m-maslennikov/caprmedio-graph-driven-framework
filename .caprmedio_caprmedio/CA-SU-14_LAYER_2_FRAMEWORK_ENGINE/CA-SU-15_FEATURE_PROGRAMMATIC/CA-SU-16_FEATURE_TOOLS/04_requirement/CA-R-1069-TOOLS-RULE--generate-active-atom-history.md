the framework **must** provide one deterministic Tool that generates the project-wide `biz_atoms_active_history.md` Projection with the number of active Atoms for **every** reporting date as a total **and** as separate counts for **every** canonical Type, structural level, **and** structural unit at that date's authoritative source frontier.
