ENGINE Spec refinement stage 2

## Objective

Group and refine the Engine specification, correct source inconsistencies through reviewed Drafts, adopt accepted changes, then use Code Synopses to prepare a separate implementation Epic. This cycle covers newly found Atoms and the general eligible Engine corpus.

## Scope

All Current and Draft ENGINE Atoms are in the inventory, including PROGRAMMATIC and APPS local sources, AGENTIC, MCP, WORKBENCH and eligible descendants. Include sources absent from query_run and sources whose declared content is not exposed. All eligible RMEDO receive the applicable review cycle. MED topic grouping focuses on justified Engineering Aspects; Role and Subject remain independent. Active A/Code Synopsis supplies implementation evidence. Other Roles remain included for accessibility and connected source corrections.

Older non-Workbench Tools and the explicitly deferred Codex plugin work stay deferred. Do not exclude all CODEX_EXTENSION-owned sources by name: shared Workbench query/View/Workspace/service contracts and the generic plugin-free hook remain included even when their existing ownership or physical location is wrong. Determine membership per Atom in a mixed Scope Unit. Preserve benchmark fixtures and existing Archived sources.

The 69-MED hold is lifted. No eligible source is excluded merely because query_run cannot read it. Record source-layout, reader-admission, schema and invalid-data issues separately. Explicitly authorized structure-only in-place edits preserve wording, properties, Versions and timestamps; semantic, Role/Form, merge, Scope and Tier corrections use Drafts and normal adoption.

## Inputs

- Current model, principles and the latest Operator input; the latest explicit correction takes precedence over earlier input.
- Role Declarations, Scope Delegations/Contracts, relation and graph constraints, and current numeric Tier rules.
- Direct Policy, Approach Policy, Solution and Decision Form Declarations R3002–R3005; DRY, MECE and the accepted M513 merge policy.
- The complete Current/Draft inventory and thin tracking from the recent source-accessibility work, including the 69 repaired MED Atoms and other query-inaccessible sources.
- Current prepared Drafts and completed review receipts. Earlier completed batches are evidence, not proof of complete coverage of this expanded cycle.

## Procedure

Before each Task or subtask, review its inputs, scope and acceptance check and fix preparation gaps. Work in same-Scope batches of at most 15–20 minutes; repeat or split batches as needed. Use Terra and Luna where efficient, with no more than one Scope Unit per subagent. Keep main judgments and integration with the primary reviewer. Independent read-only lanes may run in parallel; source mutations have exclusive ownership.

For every review point: review pass one → fix → independent pass two → fix/recheck. Revisit affected earlier checks after a split, merge, Role, Form, Scope or relation change. Run the combined cycle again before adoption. Reuse prior results only when the relevant source, governing inputs and criterion remain unchanged; record that reuse explicitly.

If unsure, re-read the model, principles and latest Operator input. If still unsure, make the best bounded proposal, create a C/Question with context, proposed treatment and blast radius, then continue independent work. Do not invent authority or create a blanket hold.

## Source work and tracking

Tasks describe work and acceptance. Specification and design belong in RMEDO, with proposals where needed. Create or refine the affected Draft as soon as a correction is concrete. Use thin tracking: Atom ID | old subject | new subject | status, plus a short Draft/Task index where needed. Do not create large mapping/specification files.

For each worked Task, keep a small P/Work Report with actual changes, checks, results and open issues. Track actual prerequisites with IS_BLOCKED_BY, allowing independent lanes to proceed. Do not duplicate inverse containment/blocking lists in Headers.

Review main subjects and dependencies; actual Claim versus Role; Scope ownership and promotion/demotion; practical one-claim boundaries with the Declaration exception; MECE; DRY; Forms; relations including connected Rs; applicable MED policies; and numeric Tiers. Apply current definitions and Operator corrections through source Drafts rather than turning this Plan into a second specification.
