## Summary

Distinguish Scope Unit Declarations from observations

## Claim

### Prose Claim

An Evaluation fails when a consumer treats an observed folder, Goal or Scope Delegation as a Scope Unit Declaration, conceals a declared Scope Unit because its Carrier or Goal is absent, or repairs contradictory sources without authorization. A missing Carrier or Goal does not erase its structural Atom or authorize a mutation.
