## Summary

Project-wide foundation alignment

## Claim

for **every** given scope, the complete active Atom set is aligned with **every** active Project-wide foundational invariant.

## Check

load the complete active Project-wide foundational invariant set first, resolve the scope, collect the complete active Atom set, including Methodology source Atoms within the selected Scope, **and** evaluate **every** Atom against **every** Project-wide foundational invariant **before** applying lower-tier authority.

## Acceptance

pass **only** **when** no Atom conflicts with a Project-wide foundational invariant **and** no lower-tier interpretation weakens **or** overrides one.

## Failure

report **every** conflicting Atom **and** Project-wide foundational invariant pair; route **every** conflict between active Project-wide foundational invariants **to** the Operator under `CA-R-2425`.
