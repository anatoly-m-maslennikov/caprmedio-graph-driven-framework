## Summary

Write concise prose aligned with ASD-STE100

## Claim

### Prose Claim

Write prose aligned with ASD-STE100.
Follow the input intent and other applicable instructions.
Use one precise term per concept throughout the Atom.
State each point once. Cut filler and details the intended reader can safely infer.
Use short sentences. Use paragraphs for explanations, bullets for related points, and numbered lists for steps in order.

Before rewriting or reviewing, read the Atom's Role and Form. Use the declared Role Semantics and the applicable Form requirements. Preserve the Atom's contribution in its Role and each section's function in its Form.

**Rewrite an Atom**

1. Read the Main Subject, Summary, Claim or other primary section required by the Atom's Form, Dependencies, and supporting sections.
2. Identify the main contribution. Keep clear statements as they are. Split dense statements when it helps. Keep each actor, condition, exception, limit, and sequence attached to every statement it governs.
3. Identify what each supporting statement explains, qualifies, substantiates, or helps apply. Flag an independent contribution or unresolved inconsistency for review.
4. Rebuild the content in ASD-STE100-aligned prose. Follow the Atom's Form. Preserve its meaning, purpose, exact CAPRMEDIO Terms, and references except where the input calls for a change. Cut repetition where its full meaning survives under the same conditions and with the same section function. Write the Summary last as a short version of the finalized contribution.

**Review the rewrite against the exact source revision**

Have an independent reviewer check the rewrite in this order:

1. Main Subject. Check that it names the matter the Atom addresses.
2. Summary. Check that it briefly and faithfully describes the finalized main contribution.
3. Claim or primary section. Check the contribution, its scope, and the conditions that govern each statement.
4. Dependencies. Check the links against the matters the Atom relies on. Flag missing, stale, or uncertain links for review.
5. Details, Semantics, and other sections. Check each section against its purpose in the Atom's Form. Details develop the contribution. Semantics explain meanings relevant to the Atom and can extend beyond what the Claim states.

Compare the source and the rewrite in both directions. Check that every required meaning survives and every statement is supported by the source or input. Check exact Terms, references, required sections, and executable content. Cite the source and rewrite passages for each issue. Repair the smallest affected passage and recheck it. If meaning or applicability remains uncertain, escalate to the Actor who can resolve it. Cite the affected passage and state what needs a decision. Keep the source wording pending the answer.

**Batch rewrites**

When using subagents, size each batch to fit the available context. When a subagent nears its context limit, save the completed results and assign the unfinished Atoms to a fresh subagent. Give it the same instructions and the pinned inputs for those Atoms.
