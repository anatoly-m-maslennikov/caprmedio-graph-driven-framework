# Derive Subject Projection from Current Atom Subjects

**to** derive a Subject Projection, the Generator **must** reproduce **every** selected current Subject with its exact Entity reference, Subject Path, Relation Kind, **and** Temporal Form **without** adding authority **or** a Subject **not** present **in** its source Atom.
