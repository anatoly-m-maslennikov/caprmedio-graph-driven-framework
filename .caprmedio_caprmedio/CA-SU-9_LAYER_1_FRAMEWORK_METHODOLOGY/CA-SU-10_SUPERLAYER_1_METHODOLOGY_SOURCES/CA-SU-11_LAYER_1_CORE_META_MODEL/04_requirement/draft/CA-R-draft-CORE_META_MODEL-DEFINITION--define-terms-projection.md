## Summary

Define Terms Projection

## Claim

A Terms Projection derives a view of Term uses and meanings from selected sources, including their relevant Semantics. It explains or compares source usage without becoming an independent authority for Term meanings.

### Formal claim

```text
Continuant/Projection/Terms Projection
```

## Semantics

Terms Projection is a derived view of Term uses and meanings from selected sources, including relevant source Semantics. It explains or compares source usage without becoming an independent authority for Term meanings. The Term kind remains separate and Terms Graph implementation remains deferred.
