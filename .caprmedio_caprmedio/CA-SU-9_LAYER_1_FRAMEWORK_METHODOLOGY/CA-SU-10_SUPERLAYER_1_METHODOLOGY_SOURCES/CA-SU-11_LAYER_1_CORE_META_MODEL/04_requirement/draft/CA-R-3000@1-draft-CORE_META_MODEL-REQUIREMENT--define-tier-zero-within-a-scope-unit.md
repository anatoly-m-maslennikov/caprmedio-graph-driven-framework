## Summary

Define Tier Zero within a Scope Unit

## Claim

### Prose Claim

Tier `0` Atoms define their own Scope Unit internally. The Atom's Role and Form remain distinct from its Tier. A Solution, Direct Policy, Approach Policy or Decision has no fixed Tier solely because of that Form. Applicable numeric Tier and governing-Relation constraints continue to apply.
