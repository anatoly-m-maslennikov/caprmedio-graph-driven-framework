## Summary

Repair the earliest inadequate authority

## Claim

### Prose Claim


Continuous-improvement RMED authority lineage.


**before** closing a material Concern, CAPRMEDIO **must** inspect its upstream RMED lineage. **if** inadequate RMED authority caused the failure, correct the earliest inadequate active authority that contributed to the cause. An implementation-only failure **must not** require changing sound RMED authority.
