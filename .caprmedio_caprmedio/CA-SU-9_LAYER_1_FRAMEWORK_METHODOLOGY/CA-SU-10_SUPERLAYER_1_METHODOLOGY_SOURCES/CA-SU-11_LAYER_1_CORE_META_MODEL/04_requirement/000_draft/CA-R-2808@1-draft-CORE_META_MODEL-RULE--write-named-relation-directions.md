## Summary

Write named Relation directions

## Claim

### Prose Claim

`Source ((RELATION)) Target` is forward and `Target <((RELATION)) Source` is reverse writing of the same canonical Relation fact. Counts stay on written endpoints; reverse normalization swaps canonical Host/Target and count slots. Match the declared Relation name exactly. The writing grants no inverse permission and creates no second stored fact.
