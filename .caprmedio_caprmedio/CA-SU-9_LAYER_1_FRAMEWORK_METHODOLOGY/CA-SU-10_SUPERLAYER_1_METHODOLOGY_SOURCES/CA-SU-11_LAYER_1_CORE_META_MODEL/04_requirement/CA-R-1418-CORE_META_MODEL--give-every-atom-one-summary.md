## Summary

Give Every Atom One Summary

## Claim

### Prose Claim


the number **and** authority of an Atom's Summary.


Every Atom has exactly one Summary for navigation, without independent authority. A Work Report copies the Summary of its IS_WORK_REPORT_FOR target. Other Atoms derive Summary from their finalized primary contribution within its applicability under CA-R-2499.
