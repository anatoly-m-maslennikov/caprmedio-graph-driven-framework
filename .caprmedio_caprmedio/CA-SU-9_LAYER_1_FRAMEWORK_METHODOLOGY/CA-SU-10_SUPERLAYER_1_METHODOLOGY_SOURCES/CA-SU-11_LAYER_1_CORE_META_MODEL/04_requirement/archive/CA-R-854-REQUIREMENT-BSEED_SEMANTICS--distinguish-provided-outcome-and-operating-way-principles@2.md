# Distinguish action, provided-outcome, and operating-way Principles

Plan Principles state who may perform or authorize governed actions. Requirement, Delivery, and Ops Principles state what CAPRMEDIO provides: Requirement owns capability, authority, control, or invariant outcomes; Delivery owns delivered results, interfaces, representations, or usable properties; and Ops owns operational support grounded in observed reality. Method and Evaluation Principles state how CAPRMEDIO works: Method owns how work, meaning, structure, or decisions are organized, and Evaluation owns how claims, results, evidence, or reliance are checked.
