# Summary

Define Entity/Carrier

## Scope

Carrier representations of Continuants.

## Claim

A Carrier **means** a storage representation of a Continuant, distinct from what it represents. A Carrier can represent a record of an Occurrent, **not** the Occurrent itself.

## Details

CA-D-256 describes storage or representation of non-ephemeral Entities; CA-D-414 classifies Carrier.

Active CA-R-1342 currently assigns Carrier definitions and classification to Requirement, while Delivery specifies how an Entity is represented, serialized, stored or placed in a Carrier.
