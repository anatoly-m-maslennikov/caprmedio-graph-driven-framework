# Keep Change and Implementation collections at Standard

an Atom Collection **or** decomposition view grouping CAPO **or** I content **must** resolve **to** Local Tier Standard, including Workflow groupings **and** Plan Hubs.

nesting expresses composition, **not** Core **or** General authority; a Plan Directory Carrier remains a Carrier of its existing Atom under CA-D-460, **not** an independently tiered collection **or** second Atom.
