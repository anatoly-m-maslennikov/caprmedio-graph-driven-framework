# Derive relational Content role from semantic control

A directional relational Atom has the Content role required by its primary controlling meaning. Endpoint positions do not determine that role: a required outcome is Requirement, a selected way is Method, a check is Evaluation, a delivery rule is Delivery, and an enacted observation is Ops. Relational shape never changes or substitutes for that Content role.
