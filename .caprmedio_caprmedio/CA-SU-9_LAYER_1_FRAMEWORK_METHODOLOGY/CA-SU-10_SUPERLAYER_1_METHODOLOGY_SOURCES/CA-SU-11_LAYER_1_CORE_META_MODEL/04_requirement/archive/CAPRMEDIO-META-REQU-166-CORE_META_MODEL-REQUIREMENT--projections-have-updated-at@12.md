# Projections have updated at

**every** Projection has one unambiguous Updated At date-time identifying its latest completed rebuild. its governed generator **or** generation procedure **and** declared configuration identify how **to** rebuild it. a Projection records dependency provenance **only** **when** its registered job needs that information; no Projection is required **to** persist a blanket source frontier.
