## Summary

Define Aspect

## Claim scope

Aspect in the Subject model.

## Claim

An Aspect is a topic through which an Atom addresses a thing, rather than another copy of that thing.

## Semantics

Aspects and Project Model subjects are independent of Content Role and owning Scope Unit. Their vocabulary is expandable. A topic does not have to be forced into Continuant or Occurrent.
