# Define Applicable Methodology as a Compiled Projection

the Applicable Methodology **must** be a non-authoritative Projection compiled from current active Atom Revisions **in** selected Methodology Source Scope Units; Applicable Methodology is **not** a Scope Unit **and** **must not** be the Claim Scope of a Goal Atom.
