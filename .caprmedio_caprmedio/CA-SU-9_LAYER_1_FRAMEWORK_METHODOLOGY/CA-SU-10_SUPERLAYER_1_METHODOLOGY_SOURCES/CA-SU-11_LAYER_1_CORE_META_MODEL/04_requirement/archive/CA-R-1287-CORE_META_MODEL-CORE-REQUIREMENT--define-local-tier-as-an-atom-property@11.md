# Define Local Tier as an Atom Property

the Atom Property Local Tier **means** the semantic abstraction coordinate of one complete independently replaceable Atom Claim within its governing authority context, distinct from the breadth of its Claim Scope, its Content Role **and** Type, **and** the ownership of its Methodology Source; admitted combinations of Content Role **and** Local Tier remain subject **to** the applicable role-tier rules, including CA-R-1566.
