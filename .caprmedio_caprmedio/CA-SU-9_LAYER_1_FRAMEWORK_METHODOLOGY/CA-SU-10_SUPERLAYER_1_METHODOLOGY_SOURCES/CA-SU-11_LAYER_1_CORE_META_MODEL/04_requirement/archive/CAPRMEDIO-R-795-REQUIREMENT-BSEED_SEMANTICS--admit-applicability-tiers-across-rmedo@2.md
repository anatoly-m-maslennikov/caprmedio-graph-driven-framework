# Admit applicability tiers across PRMEDO

Plan, Requirement, Method, Evaluation, Delivery, and Ops are the Content roles eligible for global applicability-tier classification. Plan eligibility is limited to Types that govern actions as authority, including `action_policy`; it does not make action-point Plan Types tier-classified. Eligibility does not require every Atom in an eligible role to carry a tier.
