# Define Structural level

A Structural level is a signed grouping of peer Structural units relative to CAPRMEDIO level `0`: negative levels are upstream of CAPRMEDIO and positive levels are inside CAPRMEDIO. It is one component of a Structural coordinate and does not by itself establish parentage, order, or authority.
