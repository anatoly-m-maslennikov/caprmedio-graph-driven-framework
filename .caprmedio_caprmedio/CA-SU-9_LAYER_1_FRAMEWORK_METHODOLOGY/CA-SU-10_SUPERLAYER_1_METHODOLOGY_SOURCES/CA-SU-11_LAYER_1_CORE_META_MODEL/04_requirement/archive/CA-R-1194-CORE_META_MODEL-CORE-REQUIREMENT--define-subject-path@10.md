# Define Subject Path

a Subject Path **means** a canonical Subject Expression used by a Subject Relation **to** identify **`=1`** Entity, Action, **or** Workflow through its named Term components **and** registered qualifications. qualification retains its registered endpoint constraints; the path identifies the target **without** creating another target identity **or** becoming the Subject Relation itself.
