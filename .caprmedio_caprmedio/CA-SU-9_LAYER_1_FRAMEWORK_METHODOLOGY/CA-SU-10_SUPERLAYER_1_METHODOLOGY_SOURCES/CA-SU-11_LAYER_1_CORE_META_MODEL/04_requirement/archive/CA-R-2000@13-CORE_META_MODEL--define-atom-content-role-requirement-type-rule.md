## Summary

Define Atom/Content Role: Requirement/Type: Rule

## Claim scope

The Rule value in the qualified Requirement Type domain.

## Claim

Rule **means** the Requirement Type whose Claim states a condition, permission, prohibition or constraint on a governed structure, rather than defining what that structure is.

## Details

This registration does not retire or change existing Boundary or Constraint Requirement Types. The governed Subject determines what a Rule concerns. Formal constraints are part of the model defined by the relevant Entity Definition, without a separate Graph Rule branch or Type. Definitions and rules are distinct Requirement Type values.
