## Summary

Prohibit Demands to Later Ordered Siblings

## Claim scope

Demand Atoms owned by Ordered Scope Units.

## Claim

a Demand Atom owned by an Ordered Scope Unit **must not** target a later Ordered sibling under the same direct parent.
