## Claim scope

## Claim

Project Structure **means** the zero-direct-value Property that groups a Project's accepted Scope Unit declarations. it is the single authoritative source for those structural facts, **not** a Projection, a Goal Atom, **or** a duplicate of filesystem observations. its content is represented with the bearer Project under separately governed Delivery authority; the Property has no separate Carrier.
