# Summary

Classify evaluation policy and concrete cases

## Scope

Evaluation Atoms.

## Claim

an Evaluation Atom **must** classify its numeric Tier from its complete Claim: Tier `1` Core for a foundational evaluation invariant, Tier `2` General for an independently governable representation-independent evaluation specification **or** general case criterion **when** admitted **in** its current Scope Unit, and Tier `3` Standard when no applicable higher numeric Tier **or** explicit tier rule applies. classification **must** follow CA-R-2124 through CA-R-2132 **without** inferring its Tier from Claim Scope breadth **or** requiring a concrete check **or** test specimen for Standard. Content Role and Type do not impose a Tier restriction.
