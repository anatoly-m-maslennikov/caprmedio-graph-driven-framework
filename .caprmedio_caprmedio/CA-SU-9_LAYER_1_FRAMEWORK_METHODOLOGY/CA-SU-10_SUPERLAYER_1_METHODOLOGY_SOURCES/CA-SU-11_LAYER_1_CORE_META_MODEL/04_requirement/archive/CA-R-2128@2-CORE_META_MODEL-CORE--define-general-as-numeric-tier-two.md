# Summary

Define General as Numeric Tier 2

## Scope

the numeric Tier value 2 and its initial display name General.

## Claim

Tier `2` has the initial display name General and means the reusable-rule specification tier. A General rule may constrain permitted properties, relationships, behavior, construction, checks, or representation without becoming the particular Concern, Analysis, Plan, Workflow, Action, or Implementation it governs.

General is an owner-relative display convention, not a Content-Role or Type-to-Tier restriction. Higher numeric Tiers remain admissible when the Scope Unit admits them; governing target ordering is defined independently by CA-R-2149.
