## Summary

Admit Atoms only where a role has an atomic unit

## Claim

### Prose Claim


Content Role Type admission.


a Role admits a Form value for an Atom **only** **when** that role has an independently governed atomic unit that benefits from stable identity, admission, **and** whole-unit lifecycle.

## Details

Every Current Atom has exactly one substantive admitted Type under its Content Role. A Draft Atom may remain incomplete while being authored. A Role and its Form may have the same name. This rule does not constrain Type values to a particular numeric Tier. General-name Type fields whose admission or full Subject path is not established remain uncovered and held; this rule does not claim that all Subject paths are complete.
