# Admit Foundation and Rule Types for Operations Atoms

the admitted internal Types of Operations Atoms **must** include Foundation **and** Rule **in** addition **to** the Action, Workflow, **and** Actor Types registered by CA-R-1512.

- Foundation: an Operations Atom whose Claim establishes an operational concept, fundamental boundary, **or** the operational classification system itself. definitions **and** fundamental constraints are included; this Type is **not** limited **to** vocabulary definitions.
- Rule: an Operations Atom whose Claim establishes reusable conditions governing the use, combination, **or** execution of already established operational concepts rather than defining a particular Action **or** Workflow.

these Types classify the Atom's contribution. a Foundation **or** Rule is **not** a callable Action, Workflow node, particular Run, **or** Journal Record merely because it governs Operations. their Local Tier constraints are owned by CA-O-069. the Type domain remains open under the applicable expansion authority.
