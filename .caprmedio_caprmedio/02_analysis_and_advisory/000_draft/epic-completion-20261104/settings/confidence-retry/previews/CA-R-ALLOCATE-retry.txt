## Summary

Require a valid Workflow retry allowance

## Claim

### Prose Claim

Before an automatic fix-and-evaluate retry, the applicable Workflow must have one valid retry allowance under its governing authority. Zero permits no retry after the initial failure. A missing, invalid, or ambiguous allowance stops the retry and requires Operator disposition; it does not permit a fallback past an invalid selected source.

An allowance does not grant repair permission or bypass a confidence gate, governing approval, or an additional Operator constraint. Resolution does not reset consumed retry accounting. This Requirement does not declare a Plan or Hub retry Property, a Workflow Run Property, a settings key, a default source, or a Workflow instance.
