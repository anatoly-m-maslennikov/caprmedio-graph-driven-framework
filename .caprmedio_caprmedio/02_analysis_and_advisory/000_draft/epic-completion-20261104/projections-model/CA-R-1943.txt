## Summary

Give each Projection a stable ID

## Claim

### Prose Claim


Projection identity across rebuilds.


Every Projection has one Projection ID. Its Form and Source.Named Selection determine its ID, and its ID determines its Form and Source.Named Selection. Rebuilding the same Projection retains that ID.

## Details

- A different Form or Source.Named Selection identifies a different Projection and requires a different ID.
- Changes to source contents, source Atom Versions, the Atoms selected by the same Named Selection, generated content or Updated At do not change the Projection ID. Where Source.Atoms is recorded, each entry retains its explicit Atom Version.
- A change to the current Scope Unit name or Carrier path alone does not change the Projection ID.
- Projection ID identifies the derived view, not any source Atom. It does not make the Projection authoritative.
