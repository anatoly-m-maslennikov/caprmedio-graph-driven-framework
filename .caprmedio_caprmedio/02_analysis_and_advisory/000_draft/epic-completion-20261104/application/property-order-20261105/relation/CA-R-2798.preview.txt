## Summary

Define CFEL expression-separator syntax ;

## Claim

### Prose Claim

`;` separates complete CFEL expressions; a line break is layout only. The contiguous tokens `;>` and `<;` express Property ordering and are read as complete tokens, not as the standalone separator.
