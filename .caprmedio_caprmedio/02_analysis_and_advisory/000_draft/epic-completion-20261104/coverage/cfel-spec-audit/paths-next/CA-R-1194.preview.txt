## Summary

Define Subject Path

## Claim

### Prose Claim


A canonical Subject path identifying a governed or dependency target.




The following interpretation is required when this governed concept is used:

A Subject Path is a full canonical expression identifying one thing or topic addressed by GOVERNS or DEPENDS_ON. A Subject address uses its applicable `CAPRMEDIO_MODEL` or `CAPRMEDIO_STRUCTURE` namespace and the full target expression. `Entity` is a reusable Term, not a universal path root. A source path retains the declared operators and full qualification; a short display label is not another identity or alias.

The target is selected by the meaning addressed, not by the Atom's Local Scope Unit, Content Role, folder or Carrier. The path names a target; it is not itself the Subject Relation. A defining Atom may introduce that target, so format checks do not require prior target existence.

## Semantics

A path may address classification without declaring an Entity kind. `Atom.Header.Role` addresses the relative Property, while `Atom/(.Header.Role := Plan)` identifies a qualified Atom inheriting applicable Atom features and constraints. Neither form invents an intermediate Entity kind. Formal Claims may use local expressions such as `Atom.Header.Role`; a local expression does not replace a full Subject address. The operators follow the applicable CFEL rules; no Project-name namespace layer is introduced.
