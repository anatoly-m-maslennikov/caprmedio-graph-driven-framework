## Summary

Encode Scope Unit declaration fields

## Claim

### Prose Claim


Carry a Scope Unit Declaration as a Markdown Atom. Its frontmatter carries Role = Requirement, Form = Scope Unit Declaration and the specific Scope Unit as `subjects.active`. Its own `Header.Container` stores the Declaration Atom's container as the full Scope Unit path or base Atom ID. Its Claim carries the declared Scope Unit instance's structural facts in CFEL, including applicable `IS_CONTAINED_BY` and `CONTAINS` expressions. The Declaration Atom's container does not substitute for those facts.

The shared Scope Unit model supplies field formats and cardinalities. Project inherits its unordered state and absence of order.number and Scope Unit ID from the Project model. Folder names are derived by the shared Delivery Policy; they are not authored in a Declaration.
