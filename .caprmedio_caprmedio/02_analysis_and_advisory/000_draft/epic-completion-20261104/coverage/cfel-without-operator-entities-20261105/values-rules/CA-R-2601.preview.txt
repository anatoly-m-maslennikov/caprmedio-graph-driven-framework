## Summary

Define CFEL grouping syntax ()

## Claim

### Prose Claim

`()` groups one CFEL expression and does not create a Node or Relation. When AND and OR occur in one Boolean expression, parentheses make their grouping explicit; neither has implicit precedence over the other.
