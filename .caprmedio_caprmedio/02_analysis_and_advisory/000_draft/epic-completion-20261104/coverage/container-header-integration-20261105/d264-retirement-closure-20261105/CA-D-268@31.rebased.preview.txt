## Summary

Serialize Authored Direct Relations on Their Owning Atoms

## Claim

### Prose Claim


Authored direct semantic relations.


An Atom identifies its container once through `Header.Container`, applying that Property’s admitted value and default rules. A valid Scope Unit path or base Atom ID represents its `IS_CONTAINED_BY` Relation; the root Project Declaration’s effective `world` default creates no containment link. Do not store a second `relations.IS_CONTAINED_BY` entry. Other authored direct semantic Relations **must** be serialized once under `relations.<RELATION_KIND>` on the Atom that owns their declared direction as nonempty unordered collections of unique canonical target references. `Header.Relations` is optional when no such Relations are present. Target position and source-file order are nonsemantic; validation **must not** require sorting or order normalization. Each Relation's declared direction, endpoint validity, and other semantics remain applicable.

## Details

Serialize only the declared direct direction, never a second declaration for its derived inverse. `CONTAINS` remains a derived parent view and is not serialized on the parent. An Atom child stores its parent reference in `Header.Container`; a child-only change does not write the parent Carrier or create a parent Version. The Relation's spelling does not determine its direct direction.

This Decision does not introduce another stored containment fact or a runtime relation key.
