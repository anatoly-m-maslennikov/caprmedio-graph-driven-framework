## Summary

Author Scope Delegations and internal Scope Unit definitions

## Claim

### Prose Claim

For each non-Project Scope Unit within the Project, its direct structural Parent authors and contains exactly one external Scope Delegation. The receiving Scope Unit is the Scope Delegation’s active Subject, and its direct Parent is the Container. The direct Parent assigns the receiving unit the stated Requirement, Method, Evaluation, Delivery or Operations contribution while retaining its wider scope and accountability.

This Method/Direct Policy provides common authoring guidance and requires no local adoption Atom. Each Scope Unit retains its own realization. Keep the Project root Declaration, Project Goal and root Axioms distinct. Parentage follows the declared model, not physical nesting.

- Keep Label independent of ordering state.
- Derive Authority and Delivery folder names from declared structure under the applicable Delivery rules.
- Use `.caprmedio_<project name>` for the Project Authority Folder.
