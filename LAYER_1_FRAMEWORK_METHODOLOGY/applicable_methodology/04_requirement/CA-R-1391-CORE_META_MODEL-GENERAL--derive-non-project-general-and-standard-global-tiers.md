# Summary

Derive non project general and standard global tiers

## Scope

General and Standard Global Tiers within non-Project Scope Units.

## Claim

within a non-Project Scope Unit, the General Global Tier **must** be one greater than its Core Global Tier **and** the Standard Global Tier **must** be one greater than its General Global Tier.
