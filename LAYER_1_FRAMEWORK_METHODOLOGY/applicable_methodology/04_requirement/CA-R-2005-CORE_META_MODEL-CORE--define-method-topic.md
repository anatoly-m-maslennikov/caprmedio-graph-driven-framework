# Summary

Define Method Topic

## Scope

the Method Subject root and its descendant targets.

## Claim

a Method Topic **means** a canonical Subject Target rooted at `Method` that
names how a Method Claim guides work. a Method Topic is neither an Entity Object
kind, a Scope Unit, nor the Method Atom that has a Subject Relation to it.

## Details

`Method` and every named descendant component are Term references under the
existing naming authority. their meaning is derived from the real Atom cases and
their Projections; this Claim does not require a new Definition Atom for each
topic. a concrete Method Topic is admitted only when an accepted source Method
Atom directly GOVERNS its exact canonical path and its Substance or Semantics
establishes that topic's meaning. a Method Topic path does not inherit a Scope
Unit, folder, programming language, owner, or applicability condition. an M Atom
may govern an Entity instead when its Claim is authoritative about that Entity.
