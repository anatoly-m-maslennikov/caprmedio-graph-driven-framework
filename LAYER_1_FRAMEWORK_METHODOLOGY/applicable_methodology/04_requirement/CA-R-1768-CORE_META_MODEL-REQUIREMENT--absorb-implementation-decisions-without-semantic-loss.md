## Summary

Absorb Implementation Decisions without semantic loss

## Claim

### Prose Claim

A Method Decision may be archived as absorbed only after at least one active Method Direct Policy or Approach Policy preserves every part of its still-necessary governed meaning for the affected realization scope. Absorption does not remove applicable governing constraints or imply automatic runtime conversion.
